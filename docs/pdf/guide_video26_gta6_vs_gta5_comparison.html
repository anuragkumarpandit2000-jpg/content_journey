3. How to Emulate GTA 6 Shader & Physics in Blender 4.x / 5.x

To replicate GTA 6 Vice City golden-hour volumetric lighting in Blender Cycles or Eevee Next:

# Blender Python Console: Enable Dynamic Physical Atmosphere
import bpy
world = bpy.context.scene.world
world.use_nodes = True
nodes = world.node_tree.nodes
nodes.clear()

# Add Nishita Sky Texture & Volume Scatter
sky = nodes.new(type='ShaderNodeTexSky')
sky.sky_type = 'NISHITA'
sky.sun_elevation = 0.26  # Golden Hour Vice City Angle
sky.air_density = 1.35
sky.dust_density = 2.10

output = nodes.new(type='ShaderNodeOutputWorld')
world.node_tree.links.new(sky.outputs['Color'], output.inputs['Surface'])
print("Vice City Cinematic Lighting Activated!")
