⚠️ Why You Receive Spooky Targeted Ads: By default, Google enables "Include voice and audio recordings" under Web & App Activity. When your phone hears trigger sounds or everyday conversations near the mic, acoustic telemetry is analyzed to map your purchase intent for advertisers.
🛡️ Part 1: How to Permanently Turn OFF Audio Recording
1

Open Google Account Settings

Go to your phone's Settings ➔ Scroll down and tap Google ➔ Tap the blue button "Manage your Google Account".

2

Navigate to Data & Privacy

In top navigation bar, swipe right and tap Data & privacy tab ➔ Scroll down to the "History settings" block.

3

Open Web & App Activity

Tap on Web & App Activity ➔ Scroll down to the "Subsettings" section where checkboxes appear.

4

Uncheck Voice & Audio

Look for Include voice and audio recordings ➔ UNCHECK it ➔ Tap Pause ➔ Tap Got it.
